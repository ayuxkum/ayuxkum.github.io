About me

I’m currently pursuing my MBA in Marketing and Finance at SDMIMD, Mysore. Originally from Mumbai, my academic journey has taken me from a B.Sc. in Information Technology at the University of Mumbai to a PGP in Financial Management at Welingkar, and now an MBA. Somewhere along the way, I realised that I enjoy working at the intersection of these three worlds.

During my MBA, I interned with the IT, Digital and Functional Deployment team at Union Bank of India. My work involved technology issue tracking, service requests, documentation and supporting digital initiatives related to the bank's Digital Contact Centre and website. But what really caught my interest was the research side of things. I explored areas such as digital banking, UPI, AI and FinTech, and researched and documented over 100 technology partners, their products and services to help the team evaluate them.

Before that, I worked as a Digital Marketing Intern at IIDE, where I got hands-on experience with SEO, SEM, content marketing and social media. From keyword research and competitor analysis to working with Google Analytics, I enjoyed understanding what drives digital performance and what the numbers reveal about user behaviour.

I'm also someone who likes building things rather than just talking about them. I've developed and hosted websites, built an e-commerce system for an MSME as my final-year project, and created dashboards using Excel and Python/Streamlit for a study of digital banking initiatives. In another project, I used surveys, interviews and field research to understand the career guidance needs of alumni from alternative education institutions and built a website to address those needs.

What am I looking for: I'm exploring opportunities in Business Analysis, Product Analysis, Operations Analysis and Management Trainee roles. I'm equally open to interesting opportunities across finance, marketing, technology, banking and consulting.
